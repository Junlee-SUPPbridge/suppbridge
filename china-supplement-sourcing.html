The Shape of the Problem

Sourcing Is Not the Hard Part. Verifying Is.

Finding a Chinese supplement supplier takes an afternoon. Working out whether the company in front of you is the right one takes a method.

Every Supplier Looks Qualified

Profiles, catalogues and pre-written capability lists are cheap to produce. What is expensive is the specific evidence that this operation can run your dosage form at your dose, at your volume, to your market's requirements.

Quotations Are Not Comparable

Two offers for "500mg curcumin capsules" can differ enormously on extract standardisation, carrier, grade, capsule material, testing scope and what documentation is included. Price alone compares almost nothing.

Documents Look Complete

A COA, a GMP certificate and a business licence can all be genuine and still leave your specific question unanswered — which batch, whose entity, which scope, tested by whom, against which method.

Nobody Represents You Locally

From abroad, language, distance, time zones and unfamiliar registries make independent checks impractical — so the supplier becomes your only source of information about the supplier.

Risk Moves After Payment

The commercial leverage you have during negotiation largely disappears once the deposit clears. That is precisely why verification has to happen before, not after.

Advice Is Rarely Independent

Sourcing platforms earn from transactions, and suppliers earn from your order. An experienced person whose only job is your project — and who does not manufacture anything — is structurally different.

Our Method

What We Actually Check

Verification is project-specific. These are the layers, in the order that removes the most risk per hour of effort.

01

Company Identity

Legal entity, registration details, business scope, and whether the name you are paying matches the name you are dealing with.

02

Manufacturer or Intermediary

Signals about where production physically happens, which processes are in-house, and what is subcontracted.

03

Licences & Certifications

Which production licence scopes and certificates apply, held by which entity, covering which product category.

04

Documentation Review

COAs, specifications and technical files checked for internal consistency and traceability to the goods offered.

05

Capability & Fit

Dosage form, dose per unit, MOQ, lead time, testing and documentation against your formula and target market.

Where a question can only be answered on site, we say so — and we can coordinate a factory visit or third-party inspection as a separate, clearly scoped step.

Being Straight With You

What We Don't Promise

Anybody selling you sourcing in China can offer a guarantee. The problem is that a guarantee you can't enforce is worth nothing to your business.

We Don't Guarantee Supplier Conduct

We can't control how a third party behaves after you pay. We can give you better information before you do.

We Don't Certify Ingredient Authenticity

Establishing what a material actually is requires analytical testing. We help specify the testing and read what comes back — we don't replace a laboratory.

We Don't Remove All Risk

We reduce risk and make it visible. Projects that ignore the residual risk are the ones that go wrong.

What we do commit to is precision: this is what was verified, this is what was not, this is the evidence, and these are the questions that still need answering before you commit.

Ingredient Sourcing

Comparing Offers on the Same Basis

Most ingredient quotes are not comparable, because they were never meant to be. Extract ratio, standardisation, assay, carrier, particle size, grade, packaging and testing scope are left implicit — and the cheapest quote is often cheapest precisely because something in that list was left out.

We turn a set of quotes into a like-for-like comparison against a specification you can defend, then collect the technical documents that back it up.

Start an Ingredient Sourcing Request

What a sourcing request covers

  • Ingredient specification definition and review
  • Supplier identification for the required grade
  • Documentation collection (spec, COA, MSDS, allergen and origin statements)
  • Sample coordination and sample documentation
  • Like-for-like comparison across offers
  • Regulatory screening for your target market
