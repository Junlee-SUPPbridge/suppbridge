How Format Gets Decided

What We Check Before Recommending a Format

The same format can carry very different cost, MOQ and regulatory profiles depending on the ingredients inside it. These are the questions we work through with you.

Dose & Actives

Can the dose physically fit? Does the active survive the process, the pH and the shelf life? Some ingredients rule a format out before cost is even discussed.

MOQ & Tooling

Minimum order quantity, tooling cost and changeover time differ sharply by format. We establish these before you design packaging around a format you cannot afford to run.

Market & Claims

The format affects what you can legally claim and how the product is classified — particularly in the EU, where novel-ingredient status matters at this stage, not later.

Tell us what the product has to do. We will tell you which formats can realistically carry it.
